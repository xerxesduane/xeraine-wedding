Come with us past the threshold

A wedding gathers people for a day. What we're building asks for something longer. Threshold is the ministry work we're giving our married life to here in Dubai — and the room isn't full yet. We're not asking you to decide anything on a webpage. We're asking for a coffee, a juice, or forty minutes on a video call.

Come alongside

Skills, hospitality, a network, a spare evening. Some of the best partnership has never involved money.

Just hear us out

No commitment, no pitch deck. Sit down with us, ask hard questions, and decide nothing that day.
