One last thing

Come with us past the threshold

A wedding gathers people for a day. What we are building asks for something longer. Threshold is the ministry work we are giving our married life to here in Dubai, and there is room for more of us.

We are not asking you to decide anything on a website. We are asking for a coffee, a juice, or forty minutes on a video call.

Pray with us

The simplest way in, and honestly the most valuable. You get our monthly letter, the real one.

Give with us

Monthly or one time. It goes into the work itself, and you will always see where it went.

Come alongside

Skills, hospitality, a network, a spare evening. The best help has often involved no money at all.

Just hear us out

No commitment and no sales pitch. Sit with us, ask hard questions, and decide nothing that day.
